Groq Developer Console — Plans
Start building for free. The Free plan gives every developer access to all
hosted open models, including Llama 4 Scout and Qwen3 32B, with rate limits of
30 requests per minute and 14,400 requests per day per model. Create an account
with your email or GitHub to get an API key; no credit card is required.
Upgrade to the Developer plan for higher limits and batch processing.
