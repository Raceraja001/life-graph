Gemini API pricing — free tier
Gemini 2.5 Flash is available on the free tier in Google AI Studio: 10 requests
per minute and 250 requests per day, free of charge. On the free tier, content
you submit is used to improve Google products. Paid tier prompts are not used to
improve products. A Google account is required to get an API key. Commercial use
is permitted under the API terms.
