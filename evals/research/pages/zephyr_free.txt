Zephyr Inference — Pricing

Zephyr Inference launched its public API on 2 September 2026. Every account starts on the Free plan.

Free plan
- 40 requests per minute
- 200,000 tokens per day across all models
- No credit card required to sign up
- Models: zephyr-mini, zephyr-chat-32b

Pro plan
- $0.30 per million input tokens, $0.90 per million output tokens
- 600 requests per minute

Prompts sent on the Free plan may be used to improve our models. Pro plan prompts are never used for training.
